Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 August 2026

We do not collect any data from you. None. Our apps have no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. They do not send anything anywhere.

This policy covers the apps published by RVA Software LLC, a Virginia limited liability company, and this website.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share personal information. There is no account to create, no email address to hand over, and no profile of you anywhere.

Our apps contain no advertising networks, no analytics or telemetry SDKs, no crash-reporting services, and no social media integrations.

What is stored on your device

Our games save your progress locally on your device using your operating system's standard app storage. For Hooptie Empire this is your game state — cash, inventory, loans and settings — and a note of whether you have seen the introduction.

This data never leaves your device. We cannot read it, we do not receive a copy of it, and there is no cloud sync or online backup. It is stored the same way any offline app stores its files.

How to delete it

Delete the app. That removes the saved data along with it. Because we never receive it, there is nothing for us to delete on our end and no request you need to send us.

Note that because saves are local only, deleting the app deletes your progress permanently, and progress does not transfer between devices.

Children

Because we collect no data at all, we collect no data from children. Our apps request no personal information from anyone, regardless of age.

What the app stores collect

Apple and Google operate the stores our apps are distributed through, and they collect their own information about downloads, purchases, and — if you have opted in through your device settings — crash and usage reports. That collection is theirs, governed by their privacy policies, and happens independently of us.

Where Apple or Google share aggregate statistics with us, such as download counts, we receive them as anonymous totals that identify no individual.

This website

We run no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts on this site and set no cookies. Our web host records standard server logs, including IP addresses, as an ordinary part of serving pages and defending against abuse.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the revised version here and update the date at the top. If a future app ever did collect information, we would say so plainly and in advance rather than quietly amending this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy can go to support@rvasoftware.com.
