Hooptie Empire — common questions

Does the game keep running when the app is closed?

Yes. That is the whole idea. The simulation advances from real elapsed time, so cars sell, loan payments come in, and bills fall due while you are away. When you come back, the game summarises what happened.

Where is my progress saved? Is it in the cloud?

Your save lives on your device only. There is no cloud sync and no account, which means your progress does not transfer between devices and is lost if you delete the app or lose the device. We would rather tell you that plainly than pretend otherwise.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The game is entirely self-contained and works with no connection at all. It never sends anything anywhere.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?

No ads, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and nothing that asks for money to speed things up.

My buyer has stopped buying cars.

Check your working capital floor in Office → Business. The automated buyer will not spend below the reserve you set, so a floor above your current cash stops it entirely. The panel shows your weekly bill next to it so you can size it sensibly.

Why is nothing selling?

Most often the asking price is too high. Traffic falls off sharply as price climbs above what a car is actually worth, and above roughly 1.6× its value it stops almost completely. Open a car on the lot and compare its price to its value. If a car has sat for weeks, a wholesaler will take it off your hands from the same screen.

I lost my save.

Because saves are local and we never receive a copy, we genuinely cannot recover one — there is nothing on our side to restore from. Deleting and reinstalling the app clears it permanently.
